We’re here to help

Oneirum is an offline dream journal for iPhone. For bugs, feedback, or privacy questions, contact us by email.

Contact

Email: help@oneiric.com

We aim to reply within 3–5 business days.

System requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 15.0 or later
  • Free storage for text, images, and sketches
  • Optional: notification permission for dream reminders; photo library access for images and saving sketches

FAQ

Where is my data stored?
On your device only, in the app’s private storage. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I back up my dreams?
Open Me → Export backup to save a JSON file. Keep copies in Files or iCloud Drive. Photos and sketches remain in the app folder on the device.

I forgot my app lock PIN.
Oneirum cannot recover your PIN. You may turn off app lock only if you still know the PIN, or remove and reinstall the app (this deletes local data unless you have a JSON export).

How do I delete all data?
Delete the app from your iPhone, or delete individual dream entries inside the app.

Import backup failed.
Ensure the file is JSON exported from Oneirum and not modified by other tools.

Reporting issues

When emailing support, please include:

  • iOS version (Settings → General → About)
  • Oneirum version (Me → About us)
  • Steps to reproduce the problem
  • Screenshots if helpful (avoid sharing private dream content unless you choose to)

常见问题

数据存在哪里？
仅保存在本机应用私有目录。详见隐私说明。

如何备份？
打开「我的」→「导出备份」获得 JSON 文件，可自行存到「文件」或 iCloud 盘。图片与涂鸦仍在应用本地目录。

忘记了应用锁 PIN？
无法找回 PIN。若仍记得 PIN 可在应用内修改或关闭应用锁；否则只能删除并重装应用（未导出 JSON 时本地数据会丢失）。

如何删除全部数据？
从 iPhone 删除应用，或在应用内逐条删除梦境。

导入备份失败？
请确认文件为 Oneirum 导出的 JSON，且未被其他工具改坏。
