隐私政策（简体中文）

Posyline（「本应用」）是一款个人花艺记录工具。我们重视您的隐私。本政策说明本应用如何处理信息。

1. 产品性质

本应用为纯本地个人工具，不提供账号注册、社交关系、在线商城或用户间数据共享功能。

2. 我们收集哪些信息

默认情况下，我们不会收集、上传或出售您的个人数据。您在应用中创建的内容（作品照片、配方、花材库、养护提醒、应用设置等）主要保存在您的设备本地。

3. 设备权限与用途

  • 相机：仅在您主动拍摄并添加作品图片时使用。
  • 相册（读取）：仅在您从相册选择图片导入作品、配方封面或花材图片时使用。
  • 相册（写入）：仅在您将导出的作品卡片保存到系统相册时使用。
  • 通知：仅在您为作品开启养护提醒时，用于在本地发送养护待办通知；您可在系统设置中关闭通知权限。

上述权限均由您主动触发；拒绝授权仅会影响对应功能，不影响其他本地功能的基本使用。

4. 网络访问

本应用不以账号同步为目的连接我们的服务器。部分内置或示例花材可能显示来自网络图库的缩略图（仅用于展示参考图）；该请求由您的设备直接访问第三方图片地址，我们不会因此获得您的个人身份资料。您自行添加的内容不会因此自动上传。

5. 数据存储与备份

  • 数据默认存储在您的 iPhone / iPad 本地应用沙盒中。
  • 您可使用「导出全部数据」生成 JSON 备份文件，自行保存或传输；从备份文件恢复仅在您明确选择文件并确认后写入本地。
  • 卸载应用可能删除本地数据，请重要内容务必自行备份。

6. 未成年人

本应用不面向 13 周岁以下儿童主动收集个人信息。若您认为未成年人向我们提供了信息，请通过 App Store 产品页上的开发者联系方式与我们沟通。

7. 政策变更

我们可能适时更新本政策，并在本页面修订「最后更新」日期。重大变更时，我们可能通过应用内说明或 App Store 更新说明等方式提示您。

8. 联系我们

如有隐私相关问题，请通过 App Store 中 Posyline 产品页提供的开发者联系方式与我们沟通。

Privacy Policy (English)

Posyline ("the App") is a personal floral journal. This policy describes how the App handles information.

1. What the App Is

The App is an on-device personal tool. It does not offer accounts, social features, online shopping, or sharing of your data with other users.

2. Information We Collect

By default, we do not collect, upload, or sell your personal data. Content you create (arrangement photos, recipes, flower library entries, care reminders, settings, etc.) is stored primarily on your device.

3. Device Permissions

  • Camera — used only when you choose to take photos for your journal entries.
  • Photo Library (read) — used only when you import images for arrangements, recipe covers, or custom stems.
  • Photo Library (add) — used only when you save exported arrangement cards to Photos.
  • Notifications — used only for care reminders you enable; you can turn off notifications in system Settings.

Permissions are requested in context. Denying a permission limits the related feature but does not block unrelated local features.

4. Network Access

The App does not sync your journal to our servers. Some built-in or sample stem entries may load thumbnail images from third-party image URLs for display only; those requests are made directly from your device. We do not receive your identity from those requests. Content you add yourself is not uploaded through this mechanism.

5. Storage & Backup

  • Data is stored locally in the App sandbox on your iPhone or iPad.
  • You may export a JSON backup and keep or transfer it yourself. Restore from file writes to the device only after you select a file and confirm.
  • Deleting the App may remove local data; keep your own backups of important content.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us via the developer contact on the App Store product page.

7. Changes

We may update this policy and revise the "Last updated" date on this page. Material changes may be communicated in the App or App Store release notes.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, please use the developer contact information on the Posyline App Store listing.
