Technical Support

Lune Sensory Box · iOS 15 or later · Last updated: November 2026

Thank you for using Lune. This page answers common questions about rituals, audio, journal, notifications, and data on your device.

Getting started

  1. Ritual — Tap a ritual card to start. Ambient sound plays while the breath ring follows your blend’s pattern.
  2. Rituals (Library) — Save custom blends (sound, scent notes, touch). Official presets can be adapted and saved.
  3. Journal — After a session, add a body note or photo. Entries stay on this iPhone or iPad only.
  4. Profile — Notifications, audio, privacy, cache, and replay onboarding.

Scent choices are for imagination only — no real fragrance is required to use a ritual.

Frequently asked questions

No sound during a ritual

  • Check the device mute switch and volume. In ritual playback, use the Volume slider.
  • In Profile → Audio, turn on Play in background if you want sound to continue when switching apps.
  • Force-quit and reopen Lune if audio stopped after an interruption (call, Siri, another app).

Reminders not appearing

  • Enable Profile → Notifications → Daily ritual reminder and set a time.
  • Allow notifications for Lune in Settings → Notifications → Lune on your device.
  • Focus modes and Screen Time can block alerts — check those settings if reminders are still missing.

Journal photos

  • Photos are optional. Lune only accesses the library when you tap to add an image.
  • Images are stored in the app sandbox, not uploaded to a server.
  • To free space, use Profile → Clear local cache (unused image cache only; journal text and blends are kept).

Saved blends or journal entries disappeared

Data is saved locally. Entries are removed if you delete them in the app, use “Delete” on a blend, or uninstall Lune. We do not host backups — consider keeping important notes outside the app if you need a copy.

Onboarding or daily ritual suggestion

  • Profile → View intro again replays the welcome tour.
  • Today’s featured ritual may change when you journal, check in, or use the app — this is expected.

Requirements

  • Compatible with iPhone and iPad running iOS 15 or later.
  • Internet is not required for core rituals and journaling.
  • No account or sign-in needed.

Health & safety

Lune is for quiet sensory leisure only. It does not diagnose, treat, or score your mental or physical health. If you have medical or mental health concerns, please talk to a qualified professional.

Privacy

We do not collect personal data from the app. For details, see your hosted Privacy Policy URL (same style as this support page).
