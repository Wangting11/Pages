Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Noctuelle

1. What we collect

Noctuelle does not collect, upload, or sell your personal data. Journal entries, custom blends, photos, and settings stay on your device only.

2. Permissions

  • Notifications: Only if you turn on daily ritual reminders — used for local alerts.
  • Photos: Only when you attach an optional image to a journal entry via the system picker; images are saved in the app sandbox.

3. Third parties

No account login, no third-party ads, and no analytics SDKs.

4. Deleting data

Use Profile → Clear local cache for unused image cache. Uninstalling the app removes all on-device data.

5. Health

Noctuelle offers sensory relaxation for leisure only. It is not medical or mental health treatment. Please consult a professional for health concerns.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, email help@lunesensory.cn.
