Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored? Is it synced to the cloud?

Palettes, swatches, sequences, and reference images stay in the app sandbox on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers. There is no iCloud or multi-device sync today. Uninstalling the app removes local data—use CSV export or saved posters if you need a copy.

Why does Tintelle need Photos access?

Read access lets you pick images from your library to sample colors. Write access lets you save exported swatch cards or palette posters to Photos. You can change permissions under iOS Settings → Tintelle → Photos.

Does CSV export back up everything?

No. CSV is a spreadsheet-friendly snapshot of swatches, palettes, and sequences. It does not include reference image files, and the App cannot restore a full library from CSV alone. Full import and backup are planned for a future update.

How do I delete all my data?

Open Tintelle → Settings (gear) → Clear all records, then confirm. This removes all records and related image files on this device.

What do Capture, Play, and Palettes do?

Capture — sample from photos, Gallery inspiration, or Manual HSV, with a shared color tray.
Play — turn a palette into a looping fullscreen color sequence (optional gentle sound).
Palettes — manage swatches and palettes, reference images, reordering, and poster export.

Is Tintelle a health or medical app?

No. Tintelle is a creative color tool. It is not a medical, mental health, or wellness application. Notes are for personal reference only.

System requirements

Tintelle requires iOS 15 or later. The UI supports iPhone and iPad in portrait and landscape. If something looks wrong or the app crashes, try force-quitting and reopening, or install the latest version from the App Store before contacting support.
