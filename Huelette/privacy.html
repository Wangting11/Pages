Thank you for using Tintelle (the “App”). Tintelle is a creative color toolkit for sampling hues from photos or manual picks, organizing palettes, and playing fullscreen color sequences. We respect your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information related to you.

1. Information we collect

By default, we do not upload your personal data to our servers. The App has no account system and does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Content you create and that is stored locally on your device may include:

  • Swatches (color values, optional names and notes)
  • Palettes, their order, and linked reference image files
  • Color play sequences and their settings
  • Copies of reference photos used for sampling (kept in the app sandbox)
  • CSV snapshots you choose to export from Settings

2. Device permissions

The App may request the following permissions when you use related features:

  • Photo Library (read) — select photos from your library to sample colors.
  • Photo Library (write) — save exported swatch cards or palette posters to Photos.

You can revoke these permissions anytime in iOS Settings. Some features may stop working, but data already stored in the App is not removed by changing permissions alone.

3. Storage and deletion

  • Library data and images are stored in the app sandbox (for example, JSON and image folders under Documents).
  • In the App, go to Settings → Clear all records to remove all local records and related images at once.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes all sandbox data unless you have backed up exports yourself.
  • CSV export includes structured records (swatches, palettes, sequences) only. It does not include a full image backup and cannot restore your entire library in one step.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not disclose your content to third parties except when:

  • You use system features such as Share, Save to Photos, or AirDrop and choose who receives the files; or
  • Disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights and safety.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Disclaimer

Tintelle is a creative color tool for building palettes and playing them as fullscreen sequences. It is not a medical, mental health, or wellness product. Notes and captions are for your personal use only.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change materially affects your rights, we will notify you through the App or by other reasonable means.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email support@huelette.com.
