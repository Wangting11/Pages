Technical Support

Posyline is a personal, on-device floral journal. This page covers common questions and how to reach us if you need help.

1. Before You Write

Most issues are resolved on your device without sending us your journal content. When you email us, please include:

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version
  • Posyline version (shown on the About screen)
  • A short description of what you expected vs. what happened
  • Screenshots if they help (optional)

Do not attach your full JSON backup unless we explicitly ask for a redacted sample. Backups may contain personal photos and notes.

2. Backup & Restore

  • Open Profile → Backup & restore → Export everything to save a JSON file to Files or cloud storage you control.
  • Use Restore from file only when you intend to replace or merge data on this device. Restore runs locally after you pick a file.
  • If export fails, check available storage and try again. If import fails, confirm the file is a Posyline backup JSON from this app.

3. Photos & Permissions

  • Photo Library access is requested only when you add or save images. If import is blocked, enable Photos for Posyline in Settings → Posyline.
  • The app does not use the camera; images come from your library only.

4. Care Reminders

  • Reminders use local notifications. Turn them on in the app and allow notifications for Posyline in iOS Settings.
  • Only one pending care task per arrangement is shown at a time; completing it schedules the next step based on your care settings.

5. Theme & Display

Theme colors are chosen under Profile → Theme colors and apply immediately. They are stored locally with your other settings.

6. Data on This Device

Your works, recipes, stems, and care tasks stay in the app sandbox. Uninstalling Posyline may delete local data. Keep exports of anything important.

7. Privacy

We do not operate user accounts or upload your journal to our servers. For details, see the in-app Privacy policy.

8. Contact Us

For technical questions, bug reports, or feature feedback:

support@posyline.com

We aim to reply within a few business days. Support is provided in English; you may write in other languages and we will do our best to help.
