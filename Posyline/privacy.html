Privacy Policy

Posyline ("the App") is a personal floral journal. We respect your privacy. This policy explains how the App handles information.

1. What the App Is

The App is an on-device personal tool. It does not offer user accounts, social features, online shopping, or sharing of your data with other users.

2. Information We Collect

By default, we do not collect, upload, or sell your personal data. Content you create—including arrangement photos, recipes, flower library entries, care reminders, and app settings—is stored primarily on your device.

3. Device Permissions

  • Photo Library (read) — used only when you import images for arrangements, recipe covers, or custom stems.
  • Photo Library (add) — used only when you save exported arrangement cards to Photos.
  • Notifications — used only for care reminders you turn on; you can disable notifications in iOS Settings.

Permissions are requested when you use the related feature. Denying a permission limits that feature but does not block unrelated local features.

4. Network Access

The App does not sync your journal to our servers. Some built-in or sample stem entries may load thumbnail images from third-party image URLs for display only. Those requests are made directly from your device. We do not receive your identity from those requests, and content you add yourself is not uploaded through this mechanism.

5. Storage & Backup

  • Data is stored locally in the App sandbox on your iPhone or iPad.
  • You may export a JSON backup file and keep or transfer it yourself. Restore from file writes to your device only after you select a file and confirm.
  • Deleting the App may remove local data. Please keep your own backups of important content.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us at support@posyline.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time and will revise the "Last updated" date on this page. Material changes may be described in the App or in App Store release notes.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, email us at support@posyline.com.
