Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Sartra is built for on-device use. Your closet photos and records stay on your iPhone unless you choose to export or back up.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how Sartra (“we”, “the app”) handles information when you use our iOS application.

2. Information we process

Most data is stored locally on your device:

  • Clothing photos and item details you add
  • Outfit combinations and diary entries (notes, dates, tags)
  • App preferences (appearance, theme, onboarding state)

We do not operate a user account system and do not receive your closet inventory on our servers by default.

3. Device permissions

  • Camera — to photograph items or diary moments when you choose.
  • Photo Library — to import images and, when you request it, save share cards or exports to your library.

You can deny permissions; core browsing of existing on-device data may still work where no new media is needed.

4. Network use

Sartra works offline for local features. When you record a diary entry, the app may request public weather forecasts from Open-Meteo to suggest a weather tag. That request typically includes approximate location coordinates you allow for weather, not your photos or closet list.

We do not sell personal data.

5. Export, backup, and deletion

Export and backup run only when you start them. You can delete items, entries, or reset app data from within Sartra. Uninstalling the app removes local data from the device subject to iOS behavior.

6. Children

Sartra is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, email us at support@sartra.com.
